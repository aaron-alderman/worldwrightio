# Full expedition authoring

Read README.md. Own identity and purpose in source/world.rel, readings/relations
in source/expedition.rel, atlas in source/atlas.rel and doors in source/gateway.rel.
Edit canonical facts and process JSON; never generate a second authoring source.
The initial personal readings and software/science activities are attributed
examples. Do not present them as the new author's experiences or evidence.
Any positive place/door count is supported. Preserve declared type/route/source
invariants and use a checked proposal for coordinated edits when appropriate.

Run `node build.mjs`, then `node --test template.test.mjs authoring-native.test.mjs`. `node serve.mjs`
previews dist. `node tooling/author.mjs proposal.json` previews/applies a checked
source proposal. Keep the seed, execution limit, native closure and admission.
Do not edit generated dist, inherit deployment identity or claim that source
disclosure is an execution trace. Use your own origin and publish configuration.
