# Author direction for reader reflection

**Date:** 5 November 2026  
**Status:** attributed author direction and composition proposals

## Direction supplied by Aaron

When asked which title to revise, Aaron asked: "Does making it less personal at stages help or soften the message?" When asked for material behind remembering care, he supplied: "Truth hurts".

He then clarified:

> It would be more meaningful to answer those generically, because at this stage it's "lesson" which means - it's not about me, it's about you. I can lead a horse to water but you need to see yourself shining back from the water - which is why the horse didn't drink. It's ok to have some fun.

This direction turns the lesson stage toward reader recognition. Further autobiographical disclosure is not a prerequisite to enriching the map. The quotations are from the current conversation; they are not fabricated external-source or message-export identities.

## Narrative distance

The composition can vary distance: a particular experience gives the question stakes, a generic scene makes room for the reader, and a return can change how the earlier scene is understood. The emotional spine remains available while the lesson addresses the visitor's own ways of seeing, caring and responding.

| Mode | Purpose | Candidate movement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal witness | Make the author's stakes particular. | Here is what I experienced and how I understood it. |
| Shared question | Let readers enter without having lived the same events. | What happens when a fact does not fit your explanation? |
| Reflective lesson | Invite readers to examine their own assumptions. | What would you notice if you looked again? |
| Playful image | Make a difficult recognition approachable and memorable. | A horse, water and a reflection; a reader may recognize themselves in the encounter. |
| Return | Bring the new perspective back to a prior scene. | Does that earlier question now mean something different? |

These modes are composition proposals. They are not a mandatory psychological progression or a claim that every reader needs the same lesson.

## Truth hurts as originating language

Retain "Truth hurts" as Aaron's phrase and a possible motif. Its role may be to introduce the discomfort of finding that reality resists a preferred account. It is not yet selected as a title, defined as a universal proposition, or installed in the reader.

A scene can invite checking the fact, examining the interpretation, or returning to an assumption. Discomfort itself does not decide which interpretation is true. The story can make room for recognition without telling a visitor which private experience they must be having.

## The horse and water image

Retain Aaron's wording before selecting an interpretation. The image is a storytelling cue rather than an asserted account of animal behaviour. A future composition might ask what the reader sees in the water and whether the reflection makes approaching it harder or easier.

The humour can arise from the distance between a familiar proverb and the unexpected reflection. The visitor gets to make the connection; the scene need not explain the joke or assign a moral score.

## Questions that can enrich the composition

Aaron subsequently clarified that the Encounter link should allow inspection throughout the journey: clues, hints and familiar syntax teach the reader or agent how to inspect the experience as it unfolds. [Learning through inspection](inspection-as-storytelling.md) preserves this direction and separates reader reflection from inspection of the system's own construction.

Further author questions should focus on the intended reader encounter. No personal incident is required to answer them.

1. What should a reader be able to recognise in themselves at this stage?
2. What is an attractive mistaken reading, and which next encounter could gently challenge it?
3. Where should humour release pressure, and where should the story simply remain quiet?
4. Which two routes should make the same scene mean different things on return?
5. What should remain unanswered so the reader can continue their own inquiry?

Answers supply attributed author intent and candidate composition changes. They do not automatically select titles, add branches, create permissions or publish content. The first [Encounter scene-title mapping](encounter-scene-title-edit.md) remains a one-property proposal; composing new lesson scenes is separately scoped work.
