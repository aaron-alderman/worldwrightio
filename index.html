Beyond a stack of pages

Give an idea
somewhere to lead.

A question opens a story. A choice leads to another view. The source stays close enough to check.

Worldwright explores a different way to share a body of work: a connected world with its own places, atmosphere and paths. Readers can begin where something catches, take a deeper trail, or stop and return.

The person and their work lead the experience. Worldwright is the supporting platform.

01 / INVITE

Start with curiosity.

Offer a concrete question and a clear first step. Let readers choose their way through.

02 / OPEN

Make the idea tangible.

A small interaction, a conversation or a different view can give an abstract thought something to hold onto.

03 / KEEP

Leave the source in reach.

Keep the difference between a story, an illustration and a research result clear. Give people a way to look further.

An unfolding platform.

You can explore the world now. Choose the features your world needs, then give them your own material.

The repository now provides selectable feature packs, explicit dependencies and page composition. Tidepool is an original field-station notebook that exercises the complete set.

A small reader can stay small. A richer world can add maps, evidence review, typed editing, shared programmes, media and motion. The source and effect permissions remain inspectable.
