# Worldwright's operating model

Agreed direction, 6 November 2026. This records the operating model and guides future decisions; it authorizes documentation and context updates only. No new features are being commissioned at this stage.

Worldwright makes its software, content and learning material freely available under their applicable licenses. People can learn, build and operate their own worlds. Payment is for hands-on help and voluntary support for sustaining the work.

## The inverted startup

The aim is to help people become capable and independent. A successful engagement leaves someone with their installation, source, agent and the understanding to continue. The initial corpus is the first example; Worldwright is the reusable pattern for other bodies of work.

The experience gives people a reason to connect an LLM and explore what matters to them. Public source supports read-only exploration through the person's existing tools. Those with a suitable coding agent can bring and manage their own agent. Setup and training help people learn to do this for themselves.

## What is free

The software, project-owned content, documentation and training material remain freely available under the [repository's applicable licenses](https://github.com/aaron-alderman/worldwrightio/blob/master/LICENSE.md). Paying for assistance or making a contribution is not a condition of access to that material. Existing third-party rights and per-file notices remain controlling.

## What people can pay for

| Payment | What it supports |
| --- | --- |
| Hands-on setup and installation | Practical work to get a person's material, tools and environment working together. |
| In-person training | Dedicated attention, guided practice and help with the person's own circumstances. The teaching content remains freely available. |
| Voluntary fair contributions | Sustaining maintenance, research, shared infrastructure and access for others. Contribute what you believe is reasonable and fair, according to your means. |

Voluntary contributions may be described as a suggested tithe. No fixed percentage, mandatory donation, access condition or preferential treatment is established by this model. Setup and training fees pay for agreed human work; voluntary contributions remain a separate choice. Rates and practical arrangements have not been set here.

## Responsibility for costs

Free software and content do not make inference, hosting, hardware or a person's existing subscriptions costless. Helping users understand and reduce their costs is part of the operating model. Encourage learning to bring and manage their own agent, and support their ability to take their work with them.

Any future managed API or hosting offer needs explicit, understandable costs, limits and funding. This document does not promise unlimited subsidized usage or launch a hosted service. Efficient inference should be evaluated with real-world tasks: successful outcomes, quality, latency and total cost, including retries and corrections. A low token price alone does not establish good value.

## Agent direction

Preserve free access to the software and content, user independence and voluntary participation. Do not infer a subscription, paywall, compulsory tithe or dependency on a managed service from this model. Do not introduce billing, public signup, automatic subdomain provisioning, API infrastructure or other product features without a subsequent request. The present instruction is to capture the model in documentation and agent context.
